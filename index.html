FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do you service?

At your address of choice in Plano, Richardson, Allen, Frisco, McKinney, Addison, Carrollton and Dallas. Nearby but not listed? Just ask.

Do I need to be home?

No drop-off or waiting around. We just need access to the vehicle, and we'll do a final walk-through with you when all services have been completed.

What's the difference between Exterior, Interior and Premium?

Exterior covers the wash, wheels and ceramic sealant. Interior is a full shampoo, vacuum and wipe-down. Premium is both in a single visit and saves you $35.

Can the price change?

Size and condition, such as heavy pet hair, odor, or paint damage can change the work involved. We always confirm the final price with you before any work begins.

How do referrals work?

You get $25 off for every friend you refer us to that end up booking an appointment, no matter the package (up to $75 off per detail).
