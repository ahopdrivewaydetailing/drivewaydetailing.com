FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be home during the service?

No. As long as we can access an outdoor water spigot, we can complete the job while you're away and send you photos when we're done.

Will pressure washing damage my driveway?

Not when it's done properly. We use surface cleaners and adjust pressure and temperature to your surface type, so we clean deeply without etching or striping.

How long before I can drive on it?

After washing, right away. After sealing, allow about 24 hours for foot traffic and 48 hours for vehicles, depending on weather.

How often should I seal my driveway?

Concrete and pavers every 2–3 years; asphalt every 2–3 years or when it starts to look gray and dry.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry full general liability insurance, and we're happy to provide proof on request.
