FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do you service?

Plano, Richardson, Allen, Frisco, McKinney and surrounding areas. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask.

Do I need to be home?

No drop-off or waiting around. We just need access to the vehicle; many customers carry on with their day while we work.

How long does a detail take?

A Premium Detail takes about 3 hours. Larger vehicles or heavily soiled interiors can take longer.

Why is the price a "starting" price?

Every vehicle is different. Size and condition, such as heavy pet hair, odor, or paint damage, can change the work involved. We always confirm the final price with you before any work begins.
